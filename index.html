HOW TO PLAY

You'll hear spatial premises like "B is North of C." Your job: was the n-back-ago premise the same kind as this one? Press MATCH or NO_MATCH.

At N=1, compare to the previous trial. At N=2, two trials back, and so on, up to N=10 for expert-level training. Typical adult working-memory span is around 4 items — anything past N=5 is exceptional.

The Da Vinci Method

While solving each premise, anchor the letter to a domain (Biology, Physics, etc.) and treat directions as semantic operators:

North = Up-shift (Generalize / Meta-Law)   ·   South = Down-shift (Specific Instance / Component)

East = Analogue (Sibling / Parallel System)   ·   West = Opposite (Counter-role / Foil)

This anchoring engages your frontal-polar cortex and turns abstract direction-matching into rich relational reasoning.

Match Rules per Mode

Mode 0 (Linear): Same single direction between two letters.

Mode 1 (Superposition): Same perpendicular direction pair (e.g., both "North and East").

Mode 2 (Tensor): One letter at the intersection of two perpendicular axes — same two perpendicular directions in same order across three letters.

Mode 3 (Homology): Comparing how two different letters transform — same inner and outer direction.

Mode 4 (Interface): Two multi-step paths and their endpoint relationship — same corner directions + same connecting direction.

Mode 5 (Algebraic Symmetry): Same "ratio of change" — same transformation type (Inversion vs Rotation) and same axis (Vertical/Horizontal/mixed) on both pairs. No identity (D→D) operators are ever generated; every premise describes a real shift.

Tips

Symbol letters (B, C, X, etc.) are distractors — only the directional structure matters. The letter A is never used (it can be confused with "a" in speech) — the pool is B, C, D, E, F, G, H, J, K, L, M, N, O, P, Q, R, S, T, U, V, W, X, Y, Z.

Use Adaptive N to let the game auto-tune difficulty (the Jaeggi protocol).

Cognitive Interference creates logical conflict between consecutive trials at TWO layers: (1) letter-level — letters from the previous trial reappear in conflicting roles (chain, role inversion, intervening-item, mode-specific chains); (2) direction-level — directions are biased toward opposite ("S is North of J" → "J is South of S"), perpendicular ("N → E"), or identical-with-letter-chain (transitive setups). Higher = harder, less drift. 75% is the recommended default.

d' (d-prime) measures discrimination ability from signal detection theory. Higher is better. 1.0 = decent, 2.0 = excellent.
