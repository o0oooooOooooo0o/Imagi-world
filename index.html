HOW TO PLAY

You'll hear spatial premises like "B is North of C." Your job: was the n-back-ago premise the same kind as this one? Press MATCH or NO_MATCH.

At N=1, compare to the previous trial. At N=2, two trials back, and so on, up to N=10 for expert-level training. Typical adult working-memory span is around 4 items — anything past N=5 is exceptional.

The Da Vinci Method

Da Vinci's notebooks pulled the swirl of water around an obstacle into the swirl of blood around heart valves and into the curl of hair in a portrait. Three universes apart, one structural insight. This is the cognitive move the directions in IMAGI-WORLD are meant to train.

Directions are not fixed labels. Do not memorize "North = generalize" or "West = opposite." That turns the game into lookup and kills relational reasoning. A direction is a positional marker whose meaning is constructed fresh from (a) the two letters you anchor, (b) the cognitive frame you build around the pair, and (c) the contrast the other directions in the trial set up by opposition.

Anchor across radical domain gaps. If B and C are letters, don't decode them both as biology. Let B be a beehive and C be a courtroom. Let L be lightning and M be mythology. The wider the cross-domain leap, the more relational work the direction operator has to do — and that work is the training target.

Each trial, build a new operator. The "same" North can mean unwitting structural inheritance in one trial, biological pattern lent to an abstract system in the next, and engineering-tension-mirrored-as-acoustic-tension in the next. The adaptivity itself is the skill.

Worked Mode 1 examples — "B is North of C"

Three different anchorings of the same premise, each producing a different "North" operator:

1. B = beehive geometry, C = courtroom seating. "North" = unwitting structural inheritance across eras. The hexagonal packing of bees predates and silently shapes how jury arrangements pack people into efficient seating.

2. B = breath, C = currency. "North" = bodily prototype lent to an abstract circulation system. Rhythmic intake-and-output of breath is the biological original that the in-and-out of money through an economy unknowingly mimics.

3. B = bridge cables, C = cathedral chant. "North" = physical-tension principle mirrored as acoustic-tension principle. The engineering math of distributed cable tension is the same shape as polyphonic voices holding each other in harmonic suspension.

Two more — "L is West of M"

4. L = lullaby, M = military cadence. "West" = same-mechanism, opposite-intent. Both use repeated rhythmic vocalization to synchronize a body to an external authority, but the lullaby disarms and the cadence weaponizes.

5. L = lichen, M = manuscript marginalia. "West" = parasitic encroachment on a host not designed for it. Both colonize the unused edges of a structure and quietly extend the structure's meaning over time.

Notice "North" meant three different things across examples 1–3, and "West" meant two different things across examples 4–5. The direction is the prompt; the operator is what you construct. That construction is what's being trained.

This adaptive anchoring engages frontal-polar cortex and turns direction-matching into the kind of cross-domain relational reasoning that transfers to analogy and matrix-reasoning tests.

Match Rules per Mode

Mode 1 (Linear): Same single direction between two letters.

Mode 2 (Superposition): Same perpendicular direction pair (e.g., both "North and East").

Mode 3 (Tensor): One letter at the intersection of two perpendicular axes — same two perpendicular directions in same order across three letters.

Mode 4 (Analogical Homology): True structural correspondence across four letters in two pairs. Form: "A is North and East of B, as C is North and East of D" — both pairs related by the same compound two-dimensional relation (a corner direction: NE, NW, SE, or SW). The relation R is the load-bearing object: forge A and B into distant domains, extract the relationship between them, hold it, then forge C and D across another domain gap and verify the same relationship lands. Because every relation carries both a vertical (North/South) and a horizontal (East/West) component, every trial exercises both axes — and the four relation classes are explored with equal weight. Match across trials is on the compound relation class: two trials correspond iff the same 2D relation holds across both their pairs. You cannot shortcut by tracking a single-axis token — you must extract and compare the full relation each time.

Mode 5 (Algebraic Symmetry): Same "ratio of change" — same transformation type (Inversion vs Rotation) and same axis (Vertical/Horizontal/mixed) on both pairs. No identity (D→D) operators are ever generated; every premise describes a real shift.

Mode 6 (Transitive Composition): Pure chain composition. Form: "D1 to D2 of X, D2 to D3 of Y, so D1 to D3 of X." Clauses 1+2 chain through the shared midpoint direction D2 — X carries the first step, Y bridges. The "so" binder names the composite the user has just constructed by composing the two transformations. Every trial is a real chain; there are no rejection trials and no false premises. Two trials match in n-back iff their chains compose to the same abstract operator class (vertical inversion, horizontal inversion, clockwise rotation, or counter-clockwise rotation) — recursive compression in action. A NO_MATCH response is counter-isomorphism detection: the user has judged that two chains do not share the same composite class.

Tips

The letters (B, C, D, etc.) are doing three jobs at once, not one. First, they're your anchor points for the Da Vinci method — where you bind a domain and build the cross-domain operator. Second, they're your recall handles — when a letter reappears in a later trial, the letter is the cue that pulls back the relational frame you constructed for it the first time. Third, they're transformation targets — when interference patterns deliberately re-present a prior letter in a new structural role (subject becomes object, anchor becomes pivot), you have to hold the old binding alongside the new one and decide whether the structure has been preserved, inverted, rotated, or replaced. The letters are not noise and they are not just placeholders for the directional pattern — they are the axis along which your prior relational construction gets re-evaluated.

Use Adaptive N to let the game auto-tune difficulty (the Jaeggi protocol).

Cognitive Interference creates logical conflict between consecutive trials at TWO layers: (1) letter-level — letters from the previous trial reappear in conflicting roles (chain, role inversion, intervening-item, mode-specific chains); (2) direction-level — directions are biased toward opposite ("S is North of J" → "J is South of S"), perpendicular ("N → E"), or identical-with-letter-chain (transitive setups). Higher = harder, less drift. The default is 60% — a balanced level that produces meaningful interference without overwhelming new users. Push toward 75–90% for expert training.

d' (d-prime) measures discrimination ability from signal detection theory. Higher is better. 1.0 = decent, 2.0 = excellent.
